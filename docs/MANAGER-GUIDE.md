# Getting the workspace ready: a one-page guide for the manager

You are about to become an Admin of the Rafael Support Foundation website. Your job is to enter the real details of the workspace. Customers cannot book yet. **Booking stays OFF until the owner switches it on.**

## 1. Your account (about 15 minutes)
1. Go to **rafaelsupportfoundation.org/register** and create your account. Use an email address you can open right now. Save your password in a password manager, not on a sticky note.
2. Open the email titled **Verify your email** and press the yellow button. Check Spam or Junk if it is not in your inbox, and tell the owner where it arrived.
3. Tell the owner you are verified. They will give you the Admin role.
4. Go to **Log in**, then to **rafaelsupportfoundation.org/admin**. The site asks you to set up **two-step login**. Install an authenticator app on your phone, scan the QR code, and type the 6-digit code.
5. The site then shows **10 recovery codes**. They are shown **once**. Save them in your password manager **and** on paper kept away from your phone. They are your way back in if you lose your phone.

## 2. Enter the details (Admin, then Settings)
Do them in this order. Each page saves straight away and is written to the audit log. Have the facts ready before you start.

1. **Bank details**: account name, the 10-digit account number and the bank. Customers will send their transfer here. The owner is emailed whenever these change. Booking cannot be switched on without them.
2. **Opening hours**: the opening and closing time for each weekday (Nigerian time). Tick Closed for days you do not open.
3. **Desks**: how many day desks and overnight desks there are.
4. **Prices**: whole naira only. **Leave a price blank if the fee is not confirmed.** A blank shows "Contact us for pricing" and cannot be booked online.
5. **Booking rules**: how long an unpaid booking is held, how far ahead people can book, and the pass rules. The defaults are sensible. Change them only if you know you need to.
6. **Overnight details**: check-in times, house rules and the private location. Only a member with a **confirmed overnight booking** ever sees these. They are never shown on the public site.

## 3. Please do NOT press
- **Turn booking on.** It is the owner's decision.
- **Mark lawyer review as done.** It is for after the lawyer has seen the final legal wording.
- Anything in **Payments** or **Bookings**. There is nothing there yet.

## 4. When you finish
Look at the **launch checklist** at the top of Settings. Some lines may show warnings, such as the email domain or the lawyer review. That is expected and is only advice. Tell the owner you are done, and which prices you left blank.

## If you are locked out
Use **Forgot password** for a password problem. If you lose your phone, sign in with your password and choose **Use a recovery code**. If both fail, tell the owner. Either Admin can reset the other's two-step login from **Admin, People**.
